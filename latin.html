Who I Am

About Me

Aahil Hasan is a senior at Shrewsbury High School (Class of 2027) and the recipient of the Harvard Book Award for the highest GPA in his class. He splits his time between the Latin classroom, the boathouse, the robotics shop, and the taekwondo dojang, and somehow finds time to run his own tutoring business on top of it all.

Whether he's leading 779 members as State President of the Massachusetts Junior Classical League, captaining Shrewsbury's Varsity Crew team, or mentoring middle schoolers through a speech and debate camp he helped found, Aahil is happiest when he's building something with other people and making sure everyone has a seat at the table. His 500+ volunteer hours have grown out of that same instinct, logged through National Honor Society service projects, free tutoring for classmates, Latin Honor Society outreach events, and community fundraisers run alongside Robotics and Speech & Debate.
